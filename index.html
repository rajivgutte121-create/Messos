Skip / Leave Entry

When a student does not come to the mess (gone home, on leave), enter only her record here. All other days are automatically taken as "present" - it is not necessary to mark every student attendance daily.

🗓 Register Grid — Many Absences at Once

Like marking ✕ in a register. Tap the box of that day next to the student: 1 tap = full day absent, 2 taps = Lunch only, 3 taps = Dinner only, 4 taps = back to present. Anything not tapped is counted as present.
🏖 Long leave (one student, from–to)

⚡ Absent Today — In One Tap

For the student who did not come, Lunch ya Dinner press (Breakfast goes with Lunch, Snacks goes with Dinner). Full day = both. Anything not pressed is counted as present. Pressing again returns to present.

📅 Attendance Record — Tap Only Absent Dates

All days are taken as present. Select the student, select the skipped meal (Lunch / Dinner / Breakfast), then tap that date in the calendar. Tap again to remove it.
